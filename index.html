How coaching works

A plan that keeps adapting with you.

We start with your goals, current ability and normal week. From there, the plan is built, tracked and adjusted around what is actually happening.

A better way to eat

Recipe Reinvention

Included in coaching

Keep the meals you love.

You should not have to abandon your favourite meals and replace them with completely different “fitness” versions. We keep the identity of the meal, then adjust ingredients, portions and preparation so it fits your goals more comfortably.

The Big Mac example

If you love a Big Mac, the answer is not automatically a Big Mac bowl. The goal is to recreate an actual burger that still feels and tastes like the meal you enjoy.

  1. 01

    Choose the meal

    You pick a favourite meal, takeaway order or family recipe you genuinely enjoy.

  2. 02

    Understand what matters

    I identify the flavour, texture and experience that must be preserved.

  3. 03

    Adjust the recipe

    Ingredients, portions and preparation change carefully while the meal stays recognisable.

  4. 04

    Cook and taste

    You judge the updated recipe as food, not just as a set of numbers.

  5. 05

    Refine it together

    We use your feedback to improve the taste, serving size or preparation.

  6. 06

    Make it repeatable

    The finished recipe becomes part of your normal nutrition system.
