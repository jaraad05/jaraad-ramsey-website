How coaching works

A plan that keeps adapting with you.

We start with your goals, current ability and normal week. From there, the plan is built, tracked and adjusted around what is actually happening.

  1. 01

    Understand you

    We begin with an initial consultation to understand your goals, training history, routine, food preferences and anything that needs to be worked around.

  2. 02

    Build the plan

    You receive individual training, nutrition targets and realistic habits designed around your experience, schedule and equipment access.

  3. 03

    Track what matters

    Workouts, food, measurements and progress photos can be recorded in your coaching platform so decisions are based on useful information, not guesswork.

  4. 04

    Adjust and progress

    Weekly reviews and ongoing accountability show what is working. Training, targets and habits are adjusted when your performance or circumstances change.

Recipe reinvention

Keep the meals you love.

Included in coaching

You should not have to abandon your favourite meals and replace them with completely different “fitness” versions. We keep the identity of the meal, then adjust ingredients, portions and preparation so it fits your goals more comfortably.

The Big Mac example

If you love a Big Mac, the answer is not automatically a Big Mac bowl. The goal is to recreate an actual burger that still feels and tastes like the meal you enjoy.

  1. 01

    Choose the meal

    You pick a favourite meal, takeaway order or family recipe you genuinely enjoy.

  2. 02

    Understand what matters

    I analyse the recipe with you and identify the flavour, texture and experience that must be preserved.

  3. 03

    Adjust the recipe

    Ingredients, portions and preparation are changed carefully while keeping the meal recognisable.

  4. 04

    Cook and taste

    You prepare the updated recipe and judge it as food, not just as a set of numbers.

  5. 05

    Refine it together

    We use your feedback to improve the taste, serving size or preparation until it feels right.

  6. 06

    Make it repeatable

    The finished recipe becomes part of your normal nutrition system and can be saved in your client platform.
